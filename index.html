The Problem

Why multiple shoes are a hassle

💰 Cost

College students need different shoes for different activities — casual walking, gym, outings, trekking. This means buying multiple pairs, increasing overall spending significantly.

📦 Storage

Multiple shoes take up a lot of space in your room or locker. Storage becomes a real problem when you have several pairs for different purposes.

♻️ Waste

When a shoe's sole damages or loses grip, the entire pair gets thrown away — even if the upper is still in perfect condition. This creates unnecessary waste.

Our Solution

SolesShift

SolesShift is an innovative footwear concept that solves all three problems with one simple idea: replaceable and interchangeable soles.

Instead of buying multiple shoes for different activities, you get one shoe with multiple sole options. Change the sole to match your need — from casual to performance to outdoor.

"One shoe. Endless possibilities. Make one pair of footwear more versatile."
